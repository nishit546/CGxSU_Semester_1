
#   Assignment 2 – Smart Camera Utility App

##   Objective

Build a **Smart Camera Utility App** using **Expo Camera** that provides a polished camera experience. Students should focus on **state management, camera lifecycle, gestures, and user experience**, rather than simply calling camera APIs.

---

#   Requirements

---

# Part 1 – Camera Initialization 

* Request camera permission.
* Display a loading indicator while the camera initializes.
* Show **"Camera Ready"** once initialization is complete.
* Prevent any camera action before initialization.

---

# Part 2 – Camera Modes 

Implement **three modes** inside the same screen.

```text
  Photo
  Video
  Scanner
```

Requirements

* Switching modes should update the camera behavior.
* Only the controls relevant to the selected mode should be visible.

Example

```text
PHOTO MODE
------------
Capture Button
Flash
Zoom

VIDEO MODE
------------
Record
Stop
Zoom

SCANNER MODE
------------
QR Scanner
Reset Scan
```

---

# Part 3 – Camera Controls 

Implement

* Flip Camera
* Flash Toggle
* Zoom Slider
* Pinch-to-Zoom

Additional Requirement

Disable Flash while using the Front Camera and display a message explaining why.

---

# Part 4 – Photo Capture 

Capture a photo with

* Selected quality
* Base64 enabled
* EXIF enabled

After capture, show

* Image URI
* Width
* Height
* Orientation
* Base64 Length

---

# Part 5 – Preview Screen 

Instead of immediately returning to the camera,

Show a preview screen.

Options

```text
Retake

Use Photo
```

Retake

→ Opens camera again.

Use Photo

→ Saves the image information inside memory (state).

---

# Part 6 – Video Recording 

Requirements

* Start Recording
* Stop Recording

While recording

Display

```text
  Recording

00:07
```

Timer should update every second.

After recording

Display

```text
Video Saved

URI
Duration
```

---

# Part 7 – QR / Barcode Scanner 

Requirements

After a successful scan

* Pause Preview
* Show scanned value
* Prevent duplicate scans

Buttons

```text
Resume Scanner

Scan Again
```

---

# Part 8 – Camera Settings Panel 

Create a bottom sheet or settings panel containing

```text
Picture Quality

○ High

○ Medium

○ Low
```

Changes should apply to the next captured image.

---

# Part 9 – Capture History 

Maintain an in-memory history of captures during the current app session.

For each captured photo or video, display:

```text
Photo

Time

Quality

URI
```

or

```text
Video

Time

Duration

URI
```

The history is **not** required to persist after restarting the app.

---

# Part 10 – Camera Lifecycle 

Handle camera state correctly.

Requirements

* Pause Preview
* Resume Preview
* Disable capture while paused
* Resume without restarting the camera
